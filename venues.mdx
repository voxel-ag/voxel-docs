title: "Venues"
description: "The DEX programs Voxel decodes and prices."
---

Voxel prices more than 100 Solana DEX programs. Every pool on a listed program is covered. There is no allowlist of pools, so a new market is routable from its first trade.

The `dex` field in a [quote](/api/quote) or [stream](/api/market-stream) carries the venue name as shown below. Where you send a venue name, for example the `dex` filter on `/v1/stream`, the CamelCase and lowercase spellings are also accepted.

<Note>
  Some market makers only accept swaps from approved callers. Voxel prices these venues at their public terms so quotes stay comparable, and routes through them when the route executes. Simulate before sending if you need certainty on a specific route.
</Note>

## Token programs

Both SPL Token and Token-2022 mints are supported. Token-2022 transfer fees are included in quotes.
