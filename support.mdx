When you write in, include:

- the endpoint and the full request, with amounts in base units,
- the error body, including `code` and `hint` when present,
- for a failed swap, the transaction signature,
- for a bad quote, the pool address from the route and the time.

Never share your private key or seed phrase. Voxel never asks for them.
